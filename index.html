Who is Danessa Myricks

Danessa Myricks is a significant figure in the beauty industry, known for her innovation towards makeup and her entrepreneurial spirit. Originally working in the medical field, the single mother of two wanted a more flexible career to support her children while still going after her dreams. With no formal training in makeup, she educated herself through extensive research and practice and eventually created her own beauty brand called 'Danessa Myricks Beauty' in 2015. Her brand is centered around inclusivity and innovation. Since she has launched she has consistently sold out many of her products worldwide and continues to inspire men and women across all ages that her brand is more than just makeup, it's about empowering individuals to express themselves creatively and confidently.
